2. UKRI Partner Compliance Matrix

Role Who Qualifies? Essential Actions Common Pitfalls Examples/Notes
Co-Applicant UK HEIs, approved research organisations Full fEC costing; named PI/Co-I; listed in NFS Wrong org code; missing Co-I; incorrect fEC Quantum case: Co-I omitted → bid rejected
Project Partner Charities/SMEs (unpaid); intl unis; public sector Letter of Support; no funds transferred; in-kind only Implied payments; weak LoS; borderline charity cases NHS sought staff funding → reclassified to subcontractor
Subcontractor Paid SMEs/charities; NHS/public sector service bodies Justification; procurement; VAT; LSBU contract No procurement evidence; lumped costs SME requesting £75k direct → procurement risk
Dual Role Very rare collab + paid service Senior sign-off; separate budget + agreements Subsidy risk; unclear effort split Mid-bid switch triggered near-compliance failure
International Non-UK HEIs/institutes No UKRI funds; LoS only Claiming costs; misclassified as Co-I French uni as Co-I → invalid; must be Project Partner

LSBU Quick Guidance

Strategic risk notes from RAD on partnership compliance.

  • Dual roles are exceptional and require institutional sign-off.
  • Paid charities/SMEs are subcontractors — use the correct procurement route.
  • Grey areas, especially international or dual roles, require early RAD consultation.
